# Provenance and Scope

The material in `method_extensions/` was developed through Codex-assisted
research, implementation, testing, and experiment orchestration. The project
owner selected the research scope, approved the implementation plans, and
retains responsibility for interpreting and presenting the results.

This disclosure does not imply that a generated implementation is correct by
construction. Claims are limited by the recorded unit tests, frozen protocols,
validation gates, held-out rules, and method-decision files.

The original repository workflow (Experiments 01--18) remains separately
identifiable. No smoke test is promoted to a formal PINN or DeepONet result.
