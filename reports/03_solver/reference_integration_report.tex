\begin{abstract}
This report documents the reference integration pass for the completed solver-validation study
reports. The pass creates one shared BibTeX file, adds verified citations to the ticket reports,
regenerates the report PDFs, and records unresolved source questions in a backlog. It does not
modify solver code, numerical results, figures, experiments, datasets, stress maps, neural-network
files, or machine-learning labels.
\end{abstract}

\section{Purpose of the Pass}

Tickets 01 through 12 intentionally deferred formal bibliography work while the solver-validation
ladder was still being built. This pass adds academic grounding after the validation evidence is
complete. Its purpose is narrow:
\begin{itemize}
  \item cite verified sources for the main theoretical and methodological claims;
  \item keep technical results and conclusions unchanged;
  \item avoid inventing metadata or fake DOI records;
  \item record unresolved source questions for later human review.
\end{itemize}

\section{Source Verification Policy}

References were added only when bibliographic metadata could be verified through Crossref/DOI
records or stable publisher-style metadata. If metadata was incomplete, conflicting, or merely a
reading lead in the project documents, the item was placed in \file{reference_backlog.md} instead
of being guessed into the bibliography.

This policy matters because the solver reports are meant to be study documents for beginner
researchers. A small verified bibliography is more useful than a long bibliography with uncertain
details.

\section{Limitations}

This pass is not a final paper literature review. It adds the minimum verified references needed
to ground the solver-validation reports. It does not decide the final paper's citation style, does
not expand every theoretical aside into a literature survey, and does not convert diagnostic
boundary or Greek outputs into production claims.

\section{Recommended Next Step}

The recommended next step is human review: confirm that the citation placement is sufficient for
the course or supervisor's expectations, resolve the backlog items that matter for the final
paper, and only then decide whether to begin downstream stress-map, dataset, or surrogate-model
work.
